# Completion and external-change core

- `rd.complete.closed-obligations`: use a canonical obligation ledger with owner, evidence, next experiment, status, and project-native missing-one-must-fail gate. Copy the route's `requiredCapabilityObligationIds` into the completion capability check floor; omission blocks closure. Zero Cleanup findings does not prove product completion.
- `rd.complete.claim-scope`: readiness, parity, leadership, market outcome, and human approval are separate claims. Every unmeasured baseline/surface cell remains open.
- `rd.external.current-authority`: external mutation needs current-turn authorization plus exact account/owner/repo/environment/branch/tag/release/asset target. Past approval, local config, or a pasted URL does not broaden authority.
- `rd.external.preflight`: capture current remote state, permissions, target identity, planned delta, rollback/recovery path, and dry-run when available before mutation.
- `rd.external.postverify`: execute the smallest exact change, then independently read back authoritative remote state and bind it to receipt/provenance. Local exit 0 is not completion.
- `rd.external.irreversible`: publishing, deleting, rotating credentials, enabling persistence, forced migration, and destructive cleanup require their own explicit scope and stronger recovery gate.
- `rd.complete.fresh-close`: after the last source/artifact/external change, rerun affected evaluators, strict review policy, obligation closure, and invocation revision verify.

Load `../capability-obligations.md`, `../completion-closure.md`, or `../external-change-gates.md` when the task needs their full contract.
